ABOUT ME

A little about me.

I’m Elina. I currently work as a Research Assistant in the DECIDE Lab, Department of Medicine, at the University of Chicago. I’m also pursuing an M.S. in Data Science at UChicago. Before coming to Chicago, I worked on AI systems and optimization at Samsung Electronics.

I like moving between theory and implementation: understanding why a method works, building it, and checking what happens with real data. My interests include ML systems, optimization, and decision-making. I enjoy following an idea all the way through, from the underlying math to software that people can actually use.

Outside of work

I’m happiest in nature and around animals. I love exploring new places with my dog, Eden, and finding quiet moments in a busy day.
